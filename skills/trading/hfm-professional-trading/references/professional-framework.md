## SMC/ICT Entry Framework (11-Step Checklist)

1. BIAS — HTF says which side has edge
2. LOCATION — Premium/Discount, not mid-range
3. LIQUIDITY — pools marked above and below
4. SWEEP/BREAK — price actually swept or broke a real level
5. DISPLACEMENT — impulsive move out of the zone
6. STRUCTURE — a meaningful CHoCH / BOS
7. RETEST — price returned to the OB / FVG / origin
8. TRIGGER — PA confirmation on the entry TF
9. INVALIDATION — where the idea is wrong (hard gate)
10. TARGET — opposing liquidity / structure
11. RISK — SL distance + lot fit account (hard gate)

## Entry Types (Ranked by Reliability)

1. **Liquidity Sweep + FVG** — highest probability (3-4x/week on gold)
2. **OB + FVG Overlap** — A+ setup when both agree on one zone
3. **Break and Retest** — gold's bread-and-butter (wait for close!) 
4. **Fibonacci Discount + FVG** — 50-79% retrace in discount zone

## Risk Management Rules

- **Per-trade risk**: 1% max (0.5% on funded accounts)
- **Daily loss limit**: 3% — stop trading for the day
- **Weekly loss limit**: 6% — downsize 50% next week
- **3-loss rule**: Pause after 3 consecutive losses
- **R:R minimum**: 3:1 nominal (2:1 after gold spread of ~$0.40)
- **Stop placement**: Beyond structural invalidation (sweep wick + 20-50 pip buffer for gold)
- **Partial exits**: 50% at 1:1 R:R, move SL to BE, let 50% run to full target

## Gold-Specific Gotchas

- 60-70% of apparent breakouts are false breaks (liquidity hunts)
- Gold over-extends 20-50 pips before reversing — stops must account for this
- DXY correlation ~-0.85 — check DXY before every entry
- News events (NFP, CPI, FOMC) = no trade ±30 min
- Spread on gold is wider than forex — nominal R:R must be higher to compensate
- Never enter mid-candle on gold — wicks are deceptive, wait for close

## Confluence Scoring (from chartwhisperer.ca)

0-2 factors = guess, stand down
3-4 factors = valid, tradeable setup with defined risk
5+ factors = rare, fully-stacked A+ setup

HTF trend is a GATE — without directional bias, verdict is STAND DOWN regardless of other factors.

## The Gatekeeper Method (from fxnx.com)

Minimum 3 technical reasons to exist (3-Point Rule):
- Major S/R level
- Candlestick confirmation pattern
- Indicator confluence (RSI/MACD)

Plus: no high-impact news within 2 hours, minimum 1:2 R:R, fixed 1% risk.
